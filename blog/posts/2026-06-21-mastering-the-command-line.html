Mastering the Command Line

21 June 2026

Linux is not just an operating system; it's a way of life for me. As a developer from Qusar, I've spent countless hours in the terminal, tweaking my configuration and learning how things work under the hood.

Why Terminal?

Speed. Efficiency. Control. These are the three pillars that keep me glued to kitty and bash. Whether I'm solving a competitive programming problem on Codeforces or managing my server, the CLI is where I feel at home.

In this blog, I'll be sharing my tips, tricks, and the occasional rant about algorithms and software engineering. Stay tuned!
